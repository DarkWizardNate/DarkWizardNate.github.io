2

Passwords

New tile, new passwordEvery tile you land on comes with a new proof password, like herblore12, even a tile you've been on before. Every screenshot has to show the password for the tile you're on. Proof with an old password gets denied, and drops from before you landed don't count.

Install the Clan Events RuneLite plugin before the start. It keeps the password on screen as an overlay, so every screenshot includes it without typing it in chat:

  1. RuneLite → Plugin Hub → search Clan Events → install.
  2. Put the current password in the plugin's settings. Change it every time you land on a new tile.
  3. Leave the Google Sheet ID and API key empty. The password overlay doesn't need them.

Not using the plugin? Type the password in your chat box before each screenshot.

3

Play a tile

Roll

/roll

Anyone on the team can roll, from the start time on. 1d6; snakes, ladders and free re-rolls happen as part of the roll. The bot posts the result and your new tile's task and password in your team channel.

Check the task

/current-tile

Your tile, the task, the exact proof requirements, the current password, and what your team is waiting on. Check it before you start grinding.

Submit

/submit

Opens a form for your current tile; you don't pick the tile. Attach your screenshots and add a note if the reviewer needs context (e.g. which teammate got the drop).

  • Every screenshot shows what the tile asks for and the current password.
  • Don't crop out the password, the drop message or the loot.
4

Reviews

An admin approves or denies each submission. Your team channel gets pinged either way.

Don't wait for reviews
Submit and roll on. You can keep rolling as long as no more than 10 of your submissions are waiting on review.
Denied, still on that tile
The bot posts the reason. Fix it and /submit again. Same password.
Denied after you rolled on
Your team is pulled back to redo that tile, using the denied proof's password. Your forward progress is saved, and you return to it once the redo is approved. Proof you submitted for later tiles still stands.
5

Board rules

Your task
The tile you end on, after any snake or ladder.
No repeat tiles
Land on a tile your team has approved or still in review and the bot re-rolls for you, until you land on a fresh tile. If that review is later denied, you're sent back to redo it.
Finishing
Reach or pass the last tile. If it has a task, you finish when that proof is approved. A finish only counts once every review your team has open is approved.
Placements
The race goes on after the first finish. Each team places in the order it finishes.
Time runs out
Finished teams rank first, in finish order. Then the furthest tile, with ties going to whoever got there first. Proof still in review doesn't move you.

Commands

  1. /board: the board with every team's token
  2. /standings: every team's tile and status

Channels

  1. board: live board and standings. Each team's token shows next to its name.
  2. log: every roll, snake and ladder as it happens

FAQ

I can't roll

/current-tile says why: proof still owed for your tile, more than 10 reviews pending, a redo owed, or the event hasn't started.

I'm on the wrong tile

Check /current-tile for a snake, ladder or redo. If it's still wrong, ping an admin in your team channel; they can move your team.

Can I use a drop I got before landing here?

No. Proof needs the password for the tile you're on, and you only get that password when you land.

Questions go to the event admins: post in your team channel. /gbg-help has a command recap.
